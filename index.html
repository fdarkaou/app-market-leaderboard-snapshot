App Store leaderboard snapshot

Read one App Store top chart as a dated snapshot: free, paid or grossing, overall or one category, in any covered storefront. Each row shows the rank move, a short rank trend, download and revenue estimate ranges, live paid ads and how many searches the app ranks top 10 for. Paste a market leaderboard response below. Nothing leaves your browser.

Optional: paste a report you saved earlier for the same chart (CLI --format json or --state file) to see which apps entered or left since then.

Where to get the JSON

The input is the market leaderboard response from PeekPanda, an iOS competitor catalogue that keeps dated App Store evidence for each app. One call returns the three overall charts, the chart you picked for a category, the market overview and signals for every app shown:

curl --get https://api.peekpanda.com/v1/market/leaderboard \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $PEEKPANDA_TOKEN" \
  -H 'x-genviral-tenancy: personal' \
  --data-urlencode 'country=us' \
  --data-urlencode 'categoryId=6013' \
  --data-urlencode 'chartType=free'

Over MCP this is the get_market tool with action leaderboard. You need a PeekPanda account and an API key; the market leaderboard reference lists every field, and leaderboards explains how to read rank changes.

How to read it

  • Ranks are dated public App Store chart observations. A move compares with the preceding stored chart day; "new" means the app was not on that day's chart. It says the position changed, not why.
  • Grossing rank is a relative position, not a revenue amount.
  • Downloads and revenue are PeekPanda monthly worldwide estimates, not measured results. "< $5k" is below the estimate floor. An empty cell means no estimate, not zero.
  • Paid ads are collected per chosen app. An empty ads cell means ads were not collected for that app, not that it runs none.
  • "Top-10 searches" counts App Store searches where the app was observed in the top 10 results. It is a position count, not search volume.
